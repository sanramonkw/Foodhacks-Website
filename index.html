Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Food Hacks healthy?

It allows you to use less oil than traditional methods, reducing calories and fat in your food — without sacrificing flavour.

Can the spray withstand high heat?

Yes — our sprays are designed to withstand normal cooking temperatures, making them ideal for pan-frying, baking, grilling, and more.

What is the delivery time?

Orders are typically delivered within 24 hours of confirmation. Timelines may vary by location and order conditions.

Are there any artificial flavors or preservatives?

None. Our products are free from artificial flavors, coloring, and preservatives — a genuinely healthy cooking option with no unwanted chemicals.

Can I use Food Hacks spray before cooking?

Absolutely. It's ideal for spritzing foods before grilling or baking to enhance flavour and prevent sticking.
