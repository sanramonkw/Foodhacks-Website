About Food Hacks

Welcome to Food Hacks, where we reinvent the art of cooking.

Discover the cooking spray that is changing the game of cooking. Designed to be your essential kitchen companion, it offers an innovative and easy solution to all your cooking needs.

At Food Hacks, we focus on simplifying the cooking process by offering healthy and easy methods. We are here to make every meal an opportunity to enjoy and experience something new in the kitchen.

Cook healthy and easy with Food Hacks spray. This product allows you to use a small amount of oil evenly, reducing mess and promoting a healthy lifestyle. Monitor your calorie intake effectively and enjoy delicious food without the need for large amounts of oil.

Food Hacks spray has several aspects that make it an attractive choice for many people:

  1. 1.

    ⁠Ideal Size: The size of the spray allows you to use it easily in the kitchen without taking up a lot of space or being heavy.

  2. 2.

    ⁠Non-stick Experience: Food Hacks spray is characterized by its ability to provide a non-stick cooking experience, reducing the need to use excessive oils or fats.

  3. 3.

    ⁠Multiple Use: It can be used for several purposes in cooking, from spraying ingredients with oil to improving the overall flavor of foods.

  4. 4.

    ⁠Low Calories: The spray helps reduce the amount of oil or fat used, which contributes to reducing the calories in food.

These features make Food Hacks spray a useful choice for those looking to improve their cooking experience in healthy and practical ways.

Our Factory

San Ramon is a leading brand from Kuwait, known for providing world-class products and services. The company has an extensive distribution network that gives it a unique competitive advantage.

San Ramon was established in 2004, launching its first division in the field of producing oil and gas products. Then, in 2012, the company entered the food and beverage market, becoming the owner and manufacturer of several famous brands such as Karak Tea, Honey Al-Shahd, Food Hacks, and Food Experts.

In 2013, San Ramon expanded its portfolio to include beauty products, followed by the opening of the cafes and restaurants division in 2015. In 2018, the company added the interior design division to its portfolio, with its continuous aspirations to expand its scope and establish new partnerships in the near future.
