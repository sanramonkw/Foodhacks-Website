Return & Refund Policy

Replacement and Refund within 14 Days

If you changed your mind about your Food Hacks purchase, you can request a replacement or refund under the following conditions:

  • Timeframe: Contact us within 14 days of purchase.
  • Condition: Make sure your product is still in its original, unopened condition.
  • Proof of Purchase: Have your receipt or proof of purchase handy.
  • Shipping Fees: Please note that shipping fees are non-refundable.

To initiate a return or exchange, simply contact our customer service team with your order details and reason for return. We will guide you through the steps to return it again.

Manufacturing defect (within 30 days)

We care about quality at Food Hacks. If you notice any manufacturing hiccups, we are here to make it right:

  • Timeframe: Let us know within 30 days of purchase.
  • Condition: The issue must be related to a manufacturing defect.
  • Proof of Purchase: Keep your receipt or proof of purchase handy.

Send us a message with your order details and a description of the issue. Our team will provide instructions on how to fix the problem, whether it’s a replacement or a refund..

To address a manufacturing issue, please contact our customer service team with a description of the problem and your order details. We will assess the issue and guide you through the next steps.
